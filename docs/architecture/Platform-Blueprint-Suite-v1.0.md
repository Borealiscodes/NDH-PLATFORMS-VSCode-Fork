### *NDH‑PLATFORMS‑VSCode‑Fork — Experimental Platform Architecture Blueprint*

---

## **🌱 1. Purpose of the Platform**

The **NDH‑PLATFORMS‑VSCode‑Fork** is the official **experimental layer** of the NDH ecosystem.  
It provides a safe, structured environment for:

- **NDH primitive prototyping**  
- **GBS transformation experiments**  
- **holonomy behavior observation**  
- **trauma‑informed UX development**  
- **expressive manifold documentation**  
- **White Paper staging**  
- **snek + dragon ecosystem support**

This platform exists to explore emergent behavior without contaminating canonical NDH‑PLATFORMS or institutional TISD.

## **🧩 3. Layer Definitions**

### **3.1 NDH Primitives Testbed**  
**Folder:** `ndh-primitives/`  
**Purpose:** Prototype NDH constructs (invariants, flows, meta‑structures) in isolation.  
**Rules:**  
- No canonical NDH logic copied from NDH‑PLATFORMS.  
- Only experimental variants allowed.  
- All prototypes must document their holonomy impact.

---

### **3.2 GBS Transformation Engine**  
**Folder:** `gbs-experiments/`  
**Purpose:** Apply GBS to platform behavior:  
- editor state  
- workspace geometry  
- extension logic  
- UX flows  
**Artifacts:**  
- transformation maps  
- experimental scripts  
- extension hooks  
**Rules:**  
- GBS here is **applied**, not canonical.  
- All experiments must log transformation outcomes.

---

### **3.3 Holonomy Observatory**  
**Folder:** `docs/governance/Holonomy-Behavior-Guide-v1.0.md`  
**Purpose:** Define how contributors detect:  
- coherence vs drift  
- domain absorption  
- cross‑layer interference  
- platform “mood”  
**Artifacts:**  
- holonomy heuristics  
- coherence metrics  
- contributor guidelines  
**Rules:**  
- All prototypes must include holonomy notes.

---

## **🧭 4. Platform Workflow**

### **Step 1 — Prototype NDH primitive**  
Work inside `ndh-primitives/`.

### **Step 2 — Apply GBS transformation**  
Use `gbs-experiments/`.

### **Step 3 — Observe holonomy**  
Document coherence, drift, and interference.

### **Step 4 — Adjust UX for trauma‑informed alignment**  
Prototype in `ux-trauma-informed/`.

### **Step 5 — Document expressive + technical insights**  
- expressive → `docs/expressive/`  
- technical → `docs/architecture/`  
- white paper → `docs/white-paper/`

### **Step 6 — Promote stable patterns**  
Only after review, migrate to NDH‑PLATFORMS canonical.

---

## **📜 5. Governance Principles**

- **Holonomy first:** coherence determines stability.  
- **Non‑dual workflows:** avoid binary logic.  
- **Trauma‑informed UX:** dignity constraints always apply.  
- **No domain absorption:** keep layers separate.  
- **Expressive clarity:** dragons + sneks are cultural tools.  
- **Canonical protection:** nothing escapes the fork without review.
