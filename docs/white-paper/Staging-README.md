---

## **1. Purpose of the Staging Surface**

The White Paper staging surface is the **non‑canonical documentation layer** of the NDH‑PLATFORMS‑VSCode‑Fork.  
Its purpose is to collect, organize, and refine:

- emergent case studies  
- cross‑layer behavioral observations  
- GBS transformation analyses  
- holonomy behavior notes  
- trauma‑informed UX insights  
- expressive‑technical interactions  
- NDH dialect formation events  

This surface is **experimental**, **observational**, and **non‑binding**.  
It does not define NDH architecture; it documents how the platform behaves.

Canonical NDH content lives elsewhere.  
This staging surface is intentionally isolated to preserve boundary integrity.

## **3. Staging Rules**

### **3.1 Non‑Canonical Status**
All staging artifacts are **non‑canonical**.  
They must never be migrated into NDH‑PLATFORMS without review.

### **3.2 Holonomy Compliance**
All staged documents must include a holonomy note referencing:

- coherence impact  
- boundary integrity  
- drift vectors  
- expressive‑technical alignment  
- trauma‑informed UX considerations  

See **Holonomy‑Behavior‑Guide‑v1.0.md**.

### **3.3 No Architectural Definitions**
Staging artifacts may **observe** architecture but may not **define** it.

### **3.4 Expressive Boundaries**
Dragons and sneks may appear, but:

- dragons express emergent behavior  
- sneks express invariants  
- neither may override architectural clarity  

### **3.5 Migration Requirements**
Artifacts may migrate to canonical NDH only if:

- holonomy is stable  
- boundaries remain intact  
- expressive content is contextualized  
- trauma‑informed UX is preserved  
- GBS transformations are coherent  
- reviewers approve the migration

## **4. Staging Workflow**

### **Step 1 — Observation**
Contributor identifies an emergent behavior, transformation, or cross‑layer interaction.

### **Step 2 — Drafting**
Contributor creates a staging document in the correct directory.

### **Step 3 — Holonomy Note**
Contributor adds a holonomy analysis referencing:

- coherence  
- boundaries  
- drift  
- expressive behavior  
- UX dignity constraints  

### **Step 4 — Review**
A reviewer checks for:

- holonomy stability  
- boundary integrity  
- NDH alignment  
- expressive clarity  

### **Step 5 — Integration**
Artifact is merged into the staging surface.

### **Step 6 — Optional Migration**
If the artifact becomes stable, it may be considered for canonical NDH.
